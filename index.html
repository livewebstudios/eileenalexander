ABOUT EILEEN

Supporting you on your path

I’m Eileen. Since 2012 I’ve helped people across Wayne, Saddle River and North Jersey find their footing again through holistic healing: Reiki, coaching, yoga and meditation.

I know what it feels like to be stuck. I’ve walked through grief I never imagined, including the loss of my daughter, and I’ve learned that peace and joy can live right alongside the memories we hold close. The tools that carried me changed my life. Sharing them with you is my purpose.

Every session is shaped around you. My Wayne office is close to Totowa and Pompton Lakes, and my room at Naturally Yoga in Saddle River is minutes from Ridgewood and Allendale. Or we can meet by Zoom, FaceTime or phone.
