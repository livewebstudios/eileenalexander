HEALING MIND, BODY & SPIRIT

What is Reiki?

Reiki is an ancient, gentle light-touch technique that helps your body do what it already knows how to do. Relax. Let go of stress. Heal.

It works on the physical, mental, emotional and spiritual levels, and it can also be done with a hovering, no-touch technique. The word translates to Universal (Rei) Life Force Energy (ki), the energy that runs through all of us and gives us life.

In a Reiki session I simply channel more of that energy to you. The flow brings body, mind and spirit back into alignment, balance and harmony.

WHY IT HELPS

Plugging in to recharge

Think of your body like a rechargeable battery. We have seven main energy centers, called chakras. When they run low, they get slow and sluggish and can’t do their job.

Our hectic lives keep many of us rushing around in fight-or-flight mode, and that stress drains our energy centers even faster. Energy gets blocked. Over time those blocks can show up as aches, pains and illness.

A Reiki treatment is like plugging into the charger. A steady flow of life force energy lets your body relax and step out of fight-or-flight, so stress and blocked energy can release. Your chakras regain their balance, and your body returns to its natural state of health and well-being.

Reiki isn’t a replacement for conventional medicine. It’s a holistic complement that works hand in hand with it, helping to ease anxiety and pain, soften side effects from treatment and support healing. You’ll find Reiki offered at hospitals including Memorial Sloan Kettering, Johns Hopkins, Columbia Presbyterian, Valley Hospital and Hackensack University Medical Center.

What to expect

You’ll rest fully clothed on a comfortable table while I place my hands lightly on or just above different areas of your body. Many people sink into deep relaxation, almost meditative. Some feel warmth or tingling, and some see colors or pictures in their mind’s eye. You might notice a shift right away, or over the days that follow. The best way to understand Reiki is to experience it.

How often should you come?

Reiki works best with consistency. I usually suggest four sessions about a week apart to open and begin balancing your body’s systems. Then we adjust to what you need. Many people come more often during a physical or emotional crisis, and many keep a monthly session to stay balanced.
