MEDITATION

Quiet the chatter, find your practice

The medical, spiritual and scientific worlds agree. Stress is a leading cause of illness, and meditation is a wonderful way to reduce it.

For many of us it’s hard to sit still and quiet the ongoing chatter in our minds. Meditation takes practice, patience and a willingness to suspend judgment of ourselves. I’ll help you explore the options and find the methods that suit you best.

My goal is for you to take your practice home and build a meditation ritual of your own. One-on-one or small group sessions, in office or virtual.
