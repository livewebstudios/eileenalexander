GRIEF COACHING

Honoring your loss, finding your new normal

Loss is a natural part of life. When the pain isn’t processed, it can hold back our growth and well-being. There are ways to process loss, embrace the memories and still live a full and joyful life.

Grief comes from so many places. The death of a child, spouse, parent, sibling or friend. Divorce. Relationships changed by addiction or other circumstances. Domestic violence and abuse. Illness. An empty nest as our children spread their wings. Having lived many of these myself, I want to help you embrace, honor and release your grief, and move toward love, peace and yes, even joy.

My greatest fear after my daughter died was that moving on meant leaving her behind. Losing the memories, the closeness of her laughter, the sound of her voice. Today I know the opposite is true. I’ve found a way to live a joyous, happy life and still hold her memory and spirit close.

I’d be honored to share my experience and the spiritual tools that brought me comfort, hope and happiness, and to walk beside you as you find your own new normal.

LIFE COACHING

Make your desires your reality

Do you feel you’re not living your life’s purpose and passion? Like it’s time to live more fully, but you’re not sure where to start?

I spent years feeling stuck and unfulfilled, looking for what was missing in other people, things and situations. When I started looking within, I found direction. Today I live a life full of love, happiness and abundance.

Whatever you desire, I can help you name it and take action. Maybe it’s a change in your relationships, career, finances or home. Maybe it’s attracting your ideal partner, breaking a habit or moving in a new spiritual direction. Each of us comes into this world with our own talents and gifts. When we tap into them and share them, we become truly empowered.

Together, we’ll

  • Get clear on your goals: where you are now and where you want to be.
  • Name the fears, beliefs and habits that are blocking you.
  • Explore ideas and processes to change your thinking and choose your next action.
  • Use Law of Attraction processes, meditation and other tools to make your desires real.

Along the way, you’ll learn to

  • Trust yourself
  • Believe in yourself
  • Know what makes your heart sing
  • Free yourself from limiting beliefs
  • Love yourself
  • Express your natural gifts
  • Celebrate your accomplishments
  • Own your yes and your no
  • Know you’re valuable, simply because you’re you
  • Hold a vision of the future you want
