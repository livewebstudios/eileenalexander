Supporting you on your path to an abundant, inspired life

I’ve always had a genuine love for people. I like to think of myself as a mirror, reflecting back the strength and goodness that’s already inside you.

Then I help you find the tools to set your baggage down. My desire is simple. To guide you to live in the present, name what you truly want and find your own peace and happiness.

I work with clients in Saddle River and across North Jersey, and anywhere by Zoom, FaceTime or phone.

WHAT LIFE HAS TAUGHT ME

Hope, healing & holding on

I’ve faced many hardships. Addiction, divorce and abusive relationships. Illness. Letting go as my children walked their own paths. The deaths of a spouse, a parent, grandparents, relatives and friends. And the greatest challenge of my life: the sudden, violent death of my 26-year-old daughter.

In the face of all of it, I learned to make choices. To move forward, embrace my grief and my fear, and hold the memories of the people I love in my heart. None of that came naturally or overnight. For years I felt stuck and unfulfilled, searching for what was missing in other people, things and situations. It drained me physically, financially, emotionally and spiritually.

When I began to look within, I found direction. People and opportunities came into my life to help me decide who I wanted to be, and how to show up in the world as that person.

My most challenging fear was moving forward after the death of my daughter. I was afraid I would somehow leave her behind if I moved on. Forget her. Lose the memories, the closeness of her laughter and the sound of her voice. I thought letting go of the grief and allowing peace and joy back into my life meant letting go of her.

Today I know that the opposite is true. As I find ways to live a joyous, happy life, I am able to fully embrace my daughter’s memory and hold her spirit close.

I’ve learned to embrace life, accept the things I cannot change and look at things like death in a different light. Changing my perspective changed my life. I got here through the loving support of others and the spiritual tools my teachers shared with me: yoga, meditation, Reiki, breathwork, the principles of the Law of Attraction and many other healing practices.

Today I live a fulfilling life full of peace, love, happiness and abundance. It is my purpose, passion and desire to share these tools with you as you create your own path toward hope and healing. I look forward to supporting you as you live your life to the fullest and share your unique gifts with the world.

My family

I have been blessed with four daughters: Nicole, who passed from the physical world on June 10, 2008, Andreia, Amanda and Dana. I also have two amazing, beautiful grandsons, Nicolas and Josiah, who light up my life.

Before diving full time into holistic healing in 2012, I spent 32 years as a paralegal, providing support and guidance to clients and co-workers.
