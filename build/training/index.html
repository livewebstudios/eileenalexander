Reiki is for everyone

Reiki is a safe, empowering energy healing technique that anyone can learn.

It promotes balance of body, mind and spirit. It can release stress, calm emotions and anxiety, ease physical symptoms, and support restful sleep, self-awareness and inner peace. Training is for anyone who wants to treat themselves and offer Reiki to loved ones: adults, children and pets.

About attunements

Attunements are rituals that awaken the Reiki energy within you, so you can channel more of it to yourself and others. They’re usually given in a workshop, where each student receives an individual attunement and then we learn together. Private attunements are available too.

Group trainings are scheduled through the year on my events and retreats page. Private and semi-private trainings (1 to 3 people) are scheduled at your convenience.
